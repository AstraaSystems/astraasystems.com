# Astraa Paid SaaS Backend Lane Paused

Status: PAUSED

Astraa is not launching the full automated SaaS backend/app lane at this stage.

Current launch focus:
- Public marketing website
- Pricing visibility
- Lead capture
- Contact/support flow
- Manual paid customer onboarding
- Manual payment/customer follow-up where appropriate
- No broad automated customer access

Paused/future lane:
- app.astraasystems.com backend runtime
- hosted Flask/Gunicorn app
- deployed Host/TLS smoke proof
- deployed CORS smoke proof
- deployed Moneris regression
- automatic payment-to-access unlock
- full self-serve SaaS customer portal

Reason:
The full SaaS app/backend automation lane is too large for the current solo-founder stage.
Astraa will prioritize marketing, customer discovery, lead capture, and controlled manual onboarding first.

Production boundary:
The public website may be used for marketing and lead capture.
The paused backend/app lane must not be treated as required for marketing launch.

Do not run deployed backend smoke/CORS/Moneris regression scripts unless the app/backend lane is intentionally resumed.
