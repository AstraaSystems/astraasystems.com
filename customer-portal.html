BCPI Factor explanation: The BCPI Factor is an index-style adjustment used to reflect market movement or escalation after the base $/sq ft benchmark is selected.

  • 1.05 means a 5% upward escalation adjustment.

Estimate Class

Astraa Estimator provides calibrated baseline estimate intelligence for early planning, budget comparison, and decision support. It is not a final bid, contract price, quantity takeoff, financing estimate, or replacement for professional construction review.

Included / Excluded

Included in this baseline
  • Calibrated hard-cost planning estimate
  • BC city/town planning multiplier
  • BCPI market movement factor
  • Quality level adjustment
  • Material, labor, and complexity factors
  • Soft cost allowance
  • Contingency allowance
  • Budget gap and scenario guidance
Excluded unless customized
  • Land acquisition cost
  • GST, taxes, and financing costs
  • Municipality-specific permit fee detail
  • Detailed quantity takeoff
  • Site-specific geotechnical conditions
  • Contractor-specific bid pricing
  • Legal, insurance, or lender requirements
  • Final contract or purchasing decision
Estimator accuracy & calibration note: Astraa Estimator currently provides baseline estimate intelligence for planning, comparison, and decision support. Estimate results should be reviewed before being used for bids, budgets, contracts, purchasing, or financial decisions. Accuracy improves as Astraa is calibrated with:
  • project type and scope details
  • regional labor and material conditions
  • trade-specific cost assumptions
  • historical estimates and actual final project costs
  • customer feedback shared through the Workspace

Estimator Math FAQ

Astraa Estimator builds your estimate in clear layers: hard construction cost, soft cost, contingency, budget comparison, and optional customer-entered allowances. The FAQ explains how each number is calculated and what should still be reviewed before final decisions.

  • Hard Cost: construction baseline from size, market, BCPI, quality, material, labor, and complexity.
  • Total Project Budget: hard cost plus soft cost and contingency.
  • Expanded Budget View: total project budget plus optional customer-entered allowances.
