BCPI Factor explanation: The BCPI Factor is an index-style adjustment used to reflect market movement or escalation after the base $/sq ft benchmark is selected.
  • 1.00 means no BCPI adjustment is applied.
  • 1.05 means a 5% upward escalation adjustment.
  • 0.98 means a 2% downward adjustment.
Statistics Canada BCPI should be used as escalation and market-movement context, not as a direct replacement for a cost guide, project bid, takeoff, or quantity surveyor review.
Important: Calibration uses public benchmark ranges as a planning anchor. Statistics Canada Building Construction Price Indexes are used as market-movement and escalation context through the BCPI Factor, not as a direct $/sq ft bid source. Results are baseline estimate intelligence for comparison and decision support, not a final bid, takeoff, contract price, or quantity surveyor review.

Public source: Statistics Canada Building Construction Price Indexes measure changes over time in contractor prices for new commercial, institutional, industrial, and residential buildings across selected Canadian CMAs including Vancouver and Victoria.
Estimator accuracy & calibration note: Astraa Estimator currently provides baseline estimate intelligence for planning, comparison, and decision support.   • project type and scope details

  • trade-specific cost assumptions
  • historical estimates and actual final project costs
