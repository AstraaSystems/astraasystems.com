How Astraa tools work together

Each tool can stand on its own, but the real advantage comes when data, feedback, usage, and reporting begin connecting across the workspace.

Estimate Create a baseline project or business planning number.
Capture Collect feedback, actuals, customer notes, and usage signals.
Compare Review estimate vs budget, actual cost, margin, or plan.
Improve Use reviewed learning loops to improve future recommendations.
Astraa tools are designed for planning, automation, visibility, and decision support. Final business, financial, legal, contract, purchasing, or project decisions should still be reviewed by the appropriate professional.
