Frequently asked questions

Clear answers help customers and search systems understand what Astraa offers.

What is Astraa Systems?

Astraa Systems is a connected business tools platform for estimating, expenses, operations, finance, commerce, data, and custom workflows.

Is Astraa only for construction?

No. Astraa Estimator is especially useful for construction and service workflows, but Astraa tools are designed for multiple business types.

Can I buy one tool only?

Yes. Astraa supports standalone tool pricing, workspace add-ons, and custom packages.

Does Astraa use AI?

Selected Astraa features may use AI-assisted technology to support organization, summaries, estimates, and workflow clarity. Customers remain responsible for final decisions.

What is Astraa Workspace?

Astraa Workspace is the connected area where trial access, estimates, receipts, expenses, records, and tool previews can come together.

Can Astraa build custom systems?

Yes. Astraa can create custom packages and tailored systems based on workflow, tool needs, reporting, and business process requirements.
