Frequently asked questions

Simple answers for customers deciding between trial, Basic, Professional, and Custom.

Why is the trial limited to 15 estimates?

The trial is designed as a Professional-style preview, not a replacement for a paid plan. It gives enough usage to evaluate Astraa while protecting the value of Basic and Professional.

Can I subscribe without using trial?

Yes. Customers can choose Basic or Professional directly and may receive a one-time 15-estimate launch bonus if eligible.

What happens if I use one email for trial and another for payment?

Your paid plan can still work, but the launch bonus may require business verification before it is applied.

Is Basic meant for serious daily use?

Basic is intentionally limited for very small teams, startups, and occasional use. Professional is recommended for active users.

Is the expense tool included?

Yes. Trial includes a limited preview, Basic includes Expense Tool Lite, Professional includes fuller tracking, and Custom can include tailored expense workflows.

Why is Custom priced separately?

Custom depends on workflow, trade, reporting, templates, integrations, and support needs. It is scoped separately by proposal or contract.
