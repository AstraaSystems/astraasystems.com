What this tool does.

Astraa Distribution is designed to help businesses review carrier workflows, routes, fleet usage, inventory nodes, and delivery operations with a low-friction setup path.

Best for

  • Distributors and fulfillment teams
  • Fleet-based businesses
  • Warehouses and inventory-heavy teams
  • Retail, delivery, and logistics workflows

Core capabilities

  • Carrier and courier workflow review
  • Fleet and asset planning direction
  • Warehouse and fulfillment node mapping
  • Route and delivery planning support
  • CSV and Excel workflow support

Trial and package path

Distribution can be presented as a read-only shadow workflow during trial planning, allowing teams to compare outputs without changing live operations.

Distribution supports workflow visibility and planning. Production integrations, data handling, and setup requirements are confirmed during onboarding.

Planned functions.

This page describes the client-facing workflow direction for Astraa Distribution.

Carrier Review

Review carrier, courier, and delivery workflow options in one place.

Fleet Planning

Support planning around vehicle use, delivery windows, and asset utilization.

Inventory Node Mapping

Review which warehouse, store, or supply point should support fulfillment.

Route Planning Support

Compare delivery direction, distance, cost, and timing considerations.

Zero-Overhaul Data Intake

Support common files such as CSV and Excel exports without forcing a full system replacement.
