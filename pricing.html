Individual tool pricing

Customers can use one Astraa tool on its own, add tools to Astraa Workspace, or request a custom package. Workspace add-ons are priced lower because they connect into your existing Astraa account, workspace, and records.

Standalone vs. Workspace add-on: Standalone tools are priced as independent products. Workspace add-ons are priced lower to show the value of using Astraa as one connected system. Most standalone tools are priced around 35% higher than the Workspace add-on price.
