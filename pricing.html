Plan Changes & Cancellation

Upgrading is easy & fair

Within 25 days of your purchase, moving to a higher plan or adding tools only costs the difference — you never pay twice for what you already have. All your work carries over automatically. Just email us.

Need to downgrade?

Within 15 days, if your needs change, email us and we'll work with you. We don't do cash refunds, but any difference becomes account credit toward your next payment or add-ons (like extra approvals — $10 for 10). We handle each request personally to keep it fair for your situation.

Leave anytime

You can cancel whenever you like — no questions, no hassle, we'll never hold you back. We'll simply ask for a little feedback on how we can improve, so we can earn your business again down the road.

To change, upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan, just email support@astraasystems.com — we're happy to help.
