What this tool does.

Astraa Commerce is designed to support product, quote, transaction, and business-to-business workflow readiness for companies that need cleaner commerce operations.

Best for

  • Quote-based sales workflows
  • Business-to-business pricing workflows
  • Teams preparing for structured transaction processes

Core capabilities

  • Catalog and package organization
  • Quote and transaction preparation
  • Regional pricing and currency workflow direction

  • System-to-system workflow readiness
