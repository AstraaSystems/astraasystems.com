Operations functions.

Astraa Operations is designed around practical business functions clients can understand and use.

General Actions

Track action items, owners, priorities, due dates, current status, notes, and blockers.

HR / Admin

Track onboarding-style actions, document follow-ups, training reminders, responsibility notes, and internal admin tasks.

Marketing Operations

Track campaign tasks, content actions, owners, channels, due dates, blocked work, and completion status.

Logistics / Field

Track field tasks, location-based follow-ups, assigned people, due dates, blocked actions, and completion progress.

Sales Follow-Up

Track lead follow-ups, customer tasks, next steps, potential value, status, and due dates.

Customer Service

Track customer issues, assigned owners, priority level, support notes, open status, and resolution progress.

The 15-day trial gives users a better feel for how Operations works inside Astraa Workspace alongside Estimator, Finance, and Expense.

How it works in Workspace.

Operations gives clients a simple way to create, assign, review, and summarize work across the business.

1. Create action Add the task, issue, follow-up, or operational item.
2. Assign owner Set who is responsible and when it should be reviewed.
3. Track status Use open, in progress, blocked, or done status to keep work visible.
4. Review summary Use summaries to support daily check-ins and business follow-up.
