What this tool does.

Astraa Vault is planned as the saved-records layer for Astraa Workspace, helping clients organize outputs, documents, summaries, and business records.

Best for

  • Businesses needing organized records
  • Teams saving Workspace outputs
  • Document-heavy workflows
  • Future reporting and review workflows

Core capabilities

  • Saved Workspace output direction
  • Business record organization
  • Document and summary storage direction
  • Workspace file structure support
  • Future reporting connection

Trial and package path

Vault will be added when ready as part of Workspace expansion. Clients can request access or include Vault in future Custom package planning.

Vault is a future Workspace tool. Storage, access, retention, and privacy details will be governed by service terms.

Planned functions.

This page describes the client-facing workflow direction for Astraa Vault.

Saved Outputs

Organize estimate, finance, expense, and operations summaries as Workspace records.

Document Organization

Prepare a structured place for files, summaries, and business documents.

Workspace History

Support review of previous outputs, records, and customer activity.

Record Preparation

Prepare saved records for future reporting and package review.

Controlled Access

Support access control through Workspace setup and package rules.
